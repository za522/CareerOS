# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CareerOS is primarily for Zain Ahmad, an Imperial College London Design Engineering graduate applying across finance, quantitative finance, trading, big tech, software, design engineering, startups, and founder work. The urgent shared release also supports one explicitly invited family collaborator reviewing tracker records and application documents from another computer.

Users work under real recruiting deadlines. They need to capture many roles quickly, notice newly posted finance opportunities, prepare a factual job-specific CV, and recover confidently from interrupted or failed work.

## Product Purpose

CareerOS replaces spreadsheets, open tabs, scattered documents, and manual checking with one evidence-backed career operating system. It is the source of truth for market opportunities, application activity, source freshness, alerts, profile evidence, CV drafts, immutable submitted materials, and collaboration history.

The release succeeds when a user can discover or capture a role, review it, track an application, prepare and export a faithful tailored CV, collaborate safely, and restore the complete workspace without losing history.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, candid.

CareerOS should reduce job-search stress without feeling sterile. It should communicate precision and momentum, while remaining honest about provenance, confidence, failures, service health, and actions that still need review.

## Anti-references

- Marketing landing pages inside the working product.
- Generic finance navy-and-gold styling.
- Decorative card grids, nested cards, oversized dashboard headlines, glass effects, or ornamental gradients.
- Dense interfaces that achieve compactness by making text too small.
- Vague AI language, silent AI edits, false success states, or hidden provenance.
- Workflows that require Terminal knowledge for ordinary setup or diagnosis.
- Fragile browser automation that bypasses access controls or pretends authenticated sources are public.

## Design Principles

- Start inside the real workflow and keep repeated actions close at hand.
- Make dense information scannable before revealing long detail.
- Show system state, dates, evidence, authorship, conflicts, and failures in plain language.
- Treat imported and generated content as proposals until explicitly accepted.
- Preserve user data and history before optimising speed or convenience.
- Make collaboration feel immediate without allowing silent overwrites.
- Keep local ownership and portability even when the hosted workspace is enabled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, labels, status announcements, and error recovery. Never communicate state by colour alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep body and control text legible at laptop and mobile sizes, including dense tracker, discovery, and three-column Application Studio views.
